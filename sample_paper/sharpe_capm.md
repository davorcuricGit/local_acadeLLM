# Capital Asset Prices: A Theory of Market Equilibrium under Conditions of Risk

## Authors
William F. Sharpe

## Keywords
Capital Asset Pricing Model, Market Equilibrium, Systematic Risk

## 1. Core Contribution & Objective
Establishes the theoretical foundation for the Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM), solving the problem of determining asset prices in a market equilibrium under risk by demonstrating that expected returns are linearly related to systematic risk.

## 2. Methodology & Framework
Theoretical derivation utilizing mean-variance analysis and utility maximization under uncertainty; assumes frictionless markets, homogeneous expectations, and rational investors.

## 3. Key Findings & Data Insights
Derives the Security Market Line (SML) equation linking expected excess returns to beta. Identifies the market portfolio as the unique tangency portfolio. No empirical data or figures are presented in this theoretical framework.

## 4. Limitations & Future Work
Relies on restrictive assumptions including no transaction costs, unlimited borrowing at risk-free rates, and homogeneous expectations among investors.

## 5. Terms
- **CAPM**: Capital Asset Pricing Model: A model that describes the relationship between systematic risk and expected return for assets.
- **Beta**: A measure of an asset's volatility in relation to the overall market portfolio.
- **Market Portfolio**: The theoretical portfolio containing all risky assets weighted by their market values.
